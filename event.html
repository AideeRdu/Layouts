Friday Night Karaoke

Date & Time: Every Friday | 7:00 PM – 10:00 PM

Location: Main Stage at Coffee Time, Winston

Unwind after a long week with our weekly Friday Night Karaoke! Sing your favorite hits, enjoy specialty hand-crafted drinks, and cheer on local talent in a warm and friendly atmosphere.

Event Overview

Whether you are a seasoned vocalist or just looking to have fun with friends, Friday Night Karaoke at Coffee Time is the place to be. Our cozy stage is equipped with studio-grade sound and over 10,000 song tracks ranging from classic rock to modern pop.

What to Expect

Expect an encouraging crowd, fantastic coffee, and endless fun. Here is how the evening unfolds:

  • Song Requests: Browse our digital catalog and submit your song via QR code at your table.
  • Specialty Menu: Enjoy exclusive late-night drink specials and fresh baked pastries.
  • Stage Time: Performers receive 1 free espresso shot or iced tea coupon after singing!

Schedule & Agenda

  1. 7:00 PM: Doors open, sign-ups begin, and coffee bar is open.
  2. 7:30 PM: First set of singers takes the stage.
  3. 8:45 PM: Intermission and barista showcase.
  4. 9:00 PM: Finale performances and group sing-alongs.

Accessibility & Participation

Our venue is fully wheelchair accessible. We provide seating options near the stage as well as quieter booth seating towards the back. Step-free stage access is available upon request.
